A typical high school journey

Your four-year overview

Core classes continue across high school. Over time, you’ll also have more room for electives and areas you want to explore.

Year 1

Grade 9

Build your foundation.

  • English I
  • Math and science
  • Social studies
  • World language
  • Physical education / health
  • Elective
Year 2

Grade 10

Continue core classes and explore.

  • English II
  • Math and science
  • Social studies
  • World language
  • Physical education / health
  • Visual or performing arts
Year 3

Grade 11

Choose more courses that interest you.

  • English III or American Studies
  • Math and science
  • Social studies or American Studies
  • Physical education / health
  • 21st Century Life and Careers
  • Elective or world language
Year 4

Grade 12

Finish requirements and plan what’s next.

  • English IV or English Core Elective
  • Physical education / health
  • Electives for your interests
  • Career Exploration / Internship may be an option

Credits and graduation progress

The course catalog does not list credits or officially assign each course to a graduation category. Update those details in My Plan and confirm them with your counselor. Category targets below estimate 5 credits per full year.

Graduation checklist

Requirements to plan around

  • English4 years
  • Physical Education and Health4 years
  • Mathematics3 years
  • Science3 yearsBiology, Chemistry, and Physics
  • Social Studies3 years
  • World Language2 years
  • Visual or Performing Arts1 year
  • 21st Century Life and Careers1 year
  • Financial Literacy½ year

Students need at least 120 credits to graduate, must be scheduled for at least 30 credits each year, and must earn at least 25 credits in their final year. Additional state assessment requirements apply.
