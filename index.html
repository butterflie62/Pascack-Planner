A useful way to count

What are credits?

Credits are points you earn by passing courses. They add up toward graduation.

5 creditsA full-year course
2.5 creditsA semester course

Graduation requires at least 120 credits. Students must be scheduled for at least 30 credits each year and earn at least 25 credits in their final year.

Course-catalog words

A quick translation

Prerequisite
A class or other requirement you need before you can take a course.
Elective
A course you choose. Some elective areas are still required for graduation.
Honors
An advanced course. Expectations and placement rules depend on the subject and course.
AP
Advanced Placement: a college-level course. Check its description for placement details and exam information.

Your graduation checklist

What the district requires

  • English4 years
  • Physical Education and Health4 years
  • Mathematics3 years
  • Science3 yearsBiology, Chemistry, and Physics
  • Social Studies3 years
  • World Language2 years
  • Visual or Performing Arts1 year
  • 21st Century Life and Careers1 year
  • Financial Literacy½ year

There are additional state assessment requirements. The guide also specifies 150 minutes of Physical Education/Health and Safety per week. Your counselor can help you check that your full schedule meets every requirement.

One year at a time

A typical four-year road map.

This is one possible outline—not a fixed schedule for every student. Your courses may differ based on placement, interests, and the plan you make with your counselor.

Year 1

Grade 9

Build your foundation.

  • English I
  • Math and science
  • Social studies
  • World language
  • Physical education / health
  • Elective
Year 2

Grade 10

Continue core classes and explore.

  • English II
  • Math and science
  • Social studies
  • World language
  • Physical education / health
  • Visual or performing arts
Year 3

Grade 11

Choose more courses that interest you.

  • English III or American Studies
  • Math and science
  • Social studies or American Studies
  • Physical education / health
  • 21st Century Life and Careers
  • Elective or world language
Year 4

Grade 12

Finish requirements and plan what’s next.

  • English IV or English Core Elective
  • Physical education / health
  • Electives for your interests
  • Career Exploration / Internship may be an option

Also plan for Financial Literacy: New Jersey requires it for graduation. Ask your counselor when to take the semester course or hybrid option.
